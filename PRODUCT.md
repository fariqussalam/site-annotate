# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Finance leads, CTOs, and founders at mid-market companies evaluating payment infrastructure. They browse on laptops during work hours, often comparing 2-3 vendors. They need to feel confidence and clarity quickly. No time for vague claims or visual noise.

## Product Purpose

A demo landing page for the `site-annotate` widget. Pretends to be Meridian, a payment infrastructure startup. The page itself is the product surface — it must look credible enough that users forget it's a demo, while providing enough real content (hero, features, social proof, CTA) to meaningfully test the annotation widget.

## Brand Personality

Confident, editorial, warm. Not cold-corporate. Not playful-startup. The tone of a company that has been around longer than it has. Three words: assured, crafted, human.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template with three identical feature cards and gradient icons
- Dark mode "because fintech looks cool dark"
- Navy blue + gold (the default fintech palette)
- Hero with big number + small label + gradient accent
- Glassmorphism, blur effects, or floating cards with shadows
- Gradient text or background-clip tricks
- Side-stripe borders on callouts

## Design Principles

1. **Trust through restraint.** Every element justifies its presence. No decorative noise.
2. **Editorial rhythm.** Vary spacing, type scale, and layout structure across sections. Same padding everywhere is monotony.
3. **Warmth in structure.** Use warm neutrals and a surprising accent color to avoid cold-corporate feel.
4. **Show, don't tell.** Use real UI patterns (dashboard screenshots, code snippets, integration cards) instead of abstract illustrations.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG 2.1 AA. Sufficient contrast, legible type, clear focus states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Sufficient color contrast for all text. No information conveyed by color alone.
